Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

In short: Nextcharge does not collect, sell or share your personal data. Your subscriptions stay on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

This policy explains how the Nextcharge app for iPhone (“Nextcharge”, “the app”), developed by Farid Rustamov (“we”, “us”), handles information.

Information you enter

Subscriptions, free trials, prices, dates, notes and settings you add are stored only on your device. We have no servers that receive this information and we cannot see it.

If iCloud Sync is available in your version of the app and you use iCloud on your device, this information may also be stored in your private iCloud account by Apple, so it can sync between your devices. It is encrypted by Apple and is not accessible to us. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Accounts

Nextcharge has no user accounts and no sign-in.

Exchange rates

To show totals in your currency, the app downloads public daily exchange rates directly from the Central Bank of the Republic of Azerbaijan (cbar.az). This request contains no personal information and none of your subscription data. Like any website, the bank’s server receives your device’s IP address as part of the connection.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are not sent through any server.

Purchases

Nextcharge Pro is purchased through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your payment details. The app checks with Apple whether Nextcharge Pro is active on your Apple Account.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools and no third-party SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites. Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous App Store statistics (such as download counts and crash reports from users who chose to share them with developers) in line with Apple’s policies.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual subscriptions in the app, or remove all data by deleting the app. If iCloud Sync is enabled, you can manage or delete synced data in your device’s iCloud settings.

Children

Nextcharge is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
